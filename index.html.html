Rules of Dodgeball: Penalties

















RULE #1 For some infractions, a referee may give a warning or stop play to caution an individual or team.
RULE #2 In other instances, a yellow or red card may be given immediately without warning. Refs have final judgment on the assignment of penalty cards.
RULE #3 Cards may also be issued retroactively, based on the situation and input from refs, league managers and/or WDS staff members.
